## Final contexts and choices

### q=10

- **frown** — Maria began to frown when she saw the broken window. Her eyebrows moved down, and her face looked unhappy. She asked who had hit the ball. Clues: `broken window`, `eyebrows moved down`, `face looked unhappy`. Choices: 顔をしかめる、眉をひそめる / 微笑む / 叫ぶ / うなずく.
- **slip** — Be careful on the wet floor, or you may slip. A man stepped on the puddle and almost fell, but he grabbed the rail. The cleaner put up a warning sign. Clues: `wet floor`, `almost fell`, `grabbed the rail`. Choices: 滑る / 跳ぶ / 立ち止まる / 曲がる.
- **guess** — I do not know the answer, but I will guess. I looked at the four choices and chose the one that seemed most likely. I was lucky and got it right. Clues: `do not know the answer`, `four choices`, `seemed most likely`. Choices: 推測する、言い当てる / 証明する / 記録する / 説明する.
- **crawl** — The baby cannot walk yet, so she began to crawl across the floor. She moved on her hands and knees toward a bright toy. When she reached it, she laughed. Clues: `cannot walk yet`, `on her hands and knees`, `toward a bright toy`. Choices: はう / 走る / 飛ぶ / 泳ぐ.

### q=11

- **as a general rule** — As a general rule, our library closes at six. However, it stays open until eight on Fridays. Check the sign before you visit. Clues: usual closing time, `However`, Friday exception. Choices: 概して、一般的に言えば / 例外なく / 偶然に / 急いで.
- **for a fresh start** — For a fresh start, Leo moved to another town and joined a sports club. He left his old problems behind. Soon, he made friends and felt hopeful about his life there. Clues: new town, old problems left behind, hopeful result. Choices: 新たなスタートとして、心機一転 / 最後の機会として / 思い出として / 以前と同じように.
- **on a specific occasion** — The museum is free for students on most days. On a specific occasion, however, visitors must pay because a special show is being held. The date is written on the website. Clues: `most days`, `special show`, stated date. Choices: 特定の機会に / 毎日 / 偶然に / 直ちに.
- **in case of emergency** — The hotel has a red phone beside the elevator. In case of emergency, guests should use it to call the front desk. A sign explains this for everyone. Clues: red phone, front desk call, instruction sign. Choices: 緊急時には、非常の場合に備えて / 通常営業中に / 予約のために / 休憩中に.

### q=12

- **on one's own** — Mika wanted to fix her bicycle on her own. Her father was away, and no one helped her while she worked. She felt proud when the wheels turned again. Clues: bicycle repair, no help, pride. Choices: 自分一人で、独力で / 他人の指示で / 順番に / 偶然に.
- **on the air** — The radio host spoke into the microphone at seven o'clock. The program was on the air, so people in their cars could hear her. She could not stop to talk privately. Clues: microphone, listeners, no private pause. Choices: 放送中で / 録音前で / 非公開で / 休止中で.
- **at a distance** — The sign looked small at a distance. When we walked closer, we could read every word. At first, the letters had been too small to read. Clues: small appearance, closer contrast, unreadable letters. Choices: 離れて、遠くから / すぐ近くで / 中央で / 内側から.
- **to one's surprise** — To one's surprise, the old clock still worked perfectly. Everyone had thought it was broken and ready to throw away. The owner smiled when it began to ring. Clues: working clock, broken expectation, reaction. Choices: （人が）驚いたことに / 予想どおりに / 念のため / 急いで.

### q=13

- **go along with** — Mina did not like the camping plan, but all her friends supported it. At last, she decided to go along with the plan and packed her bag. She joined them the next morning. Clues: initial disagreement, friends' support, joining behavior. Choices: （案・人に）賛成する、従う / 反対する / 延期する / 説明する.
- **take away from** — Loud music can take away from the movie. We could not hear the actors, so the story was hard to follow. After the music stopped, the film became enjoyable again. Clues: noise, lost dialogue, enjoyment returning. Choices: 〜の価値（魅力）を損なう / 価値を高める / 完全に理解する / 注意を向ける.
- **bring out in** — The art teacher's kind words bring out in Ken a confidence he did not show before. He began to share his paintings with the class. Later, he volunteered to show his work at the school festival. Clues: kind words, newly visible confidence, voluntary action. Choices: 引き出す / 隠す / 禁止する / 比較する.
- **watch out for** — Watch out for loose stones on this mountain path. They may roll under your feet and make you fall. Stay near the wall and walk slowly. Clues: loose stones, fall risk, careful movement. Choices: 〜に気をつける、用心する / 無視する / 追いかける / 頼る.
